## 万能人物框架：Tao

### 1️⃣ Who he is
I’d like to talk about my friend **Tao**, who grew up with me. We have known each other since childhood, and he has always been one of the most impressive people I’ve met.  

### 2️⃣ Personality & Appearance
He is a **handsome and confident guy**, always paying attention to his appearance. Sometimes, he dresses like a **K-pop idol**, wearing trendy clothes and stylish accessories. But occasionally, he wears something quite **unusual** — for example, he doesn’t care about the weather as long as his outfit looks fashionable.  

### 3️⃣ Talents & Hobbies
Tao is also **very talented in singing**. When we were in high school, he won first prize in a singing competition with a romantic song. I still remember feeling so touched when I listened to him sing. Even now, we often go to KTV together, and I really enjoy listening to his voice. 

Besides music, he’s also **passionate about basketball**. During university, he became a **member of the basketball team** and even led the team to win several championships. I really admire him because I’m not very good at basketball myself, but he often encouraged me to play and helped me gain confidence.  

### 4️⃣ Relationship & Impression
Because of his charm and talents, Tao was **very popular** among students. Maybe he was a bit proud at first, and I used to find him a little **arrogant**. But later, after spending more time together, I realized he was actually very kind and supportive, so we became **close friends**.  

### 5️⃣ Career Development & Intelligence
After graduation, he decided to **help with his family business**, which is a **convenience store**. What impressed me the most is that he **managed to shift from being an athlete to a business operator** so quickly. Even though he never studied management or business formally at university, he **taught himself everything** — from stock control to customer service — by reading books, observing experienced people, and learning from daily practice.  
Within just a few months, he became quite good at running the shop, and even introduced **new marketing ideas** that helped attract more customers. I think this shows how **intelligent and adaptable** he is.  

### 6️⃣ Feelings / Conclusion
For me, Tao is not only a talented and stylish person but also someone who **inspires me to be more confident and to keep learning**. He’s a rare combination of **creativity, intelligence, and determination**, which is why I truly admire him and consider him an important friend in my life.
