Describe a popular person You should say:

Who this person is  

* I‘d like to talk about my friend Tao, who **grew up with me**
* we have **known each other since childhood**

What kind of person he or she is 

* He is a **handsome and confident** guy, always paying attention to his **appearance**
* Sometimes, he dresses like a K-pop idol
* He is also **quite talented in singing**, when we were in high school, he **won first prize** in a singing competition with a romantic song
* Besides music, he's also **passionate about basketball**. During university, he became a member of the basketball team and even **led the team to win several championships**.
* That's why he was extremely popular among students

When you see him/her normally  

* At present, I usually meet him after work or on weekends when we **play basketball together**
* I’m not very good at basketball and singing, but he often **encourages me** to play and helps me **gain confidence**.

And explain why you think this person is popular

* I think what makes him truly popular is **not only his talent and a nice appearance**
* but also he's **positive attitude** and his ability to **encourage others**, including me, to be more confident.
