# Family business

*Describe a person you know who enjoys working for a family business (e.g. a shop, etc.) You should say:* 

*Who he/she is* 

*What the business is* 

*What his/her job is*  

*And explain why he/she enjoys working there*

## Part2 Structure

*Who he/she is*

* I'd like to mention my **cousin** who works in his **family's convenience store** in my hometown

*What the business is* 

* The store is located in a **residential area** and has **been running for over twenty years**
* It **sells daily necessities** such as snacks, drinks, household items

*What his/her job is* 

* He mainly helps with **stocking shelves**, **handing the cashier**, and **serving customers**
* **Occasionally**,he also helps his parents **manage suppliers** and **check the delivery of goods**

*And explain why he/she enjoys working there*

* He seems to enjoy working there for a few reasons
* First, he feels **a sense of responsibility** beacase the store supports his family's income
* Secondly, he **appreciates the flexibility**
* He can choose his working hours, in particular when **demand is lower**
* This advantage gives him plenty of time to do things he likes
* such as **going fishing**, **investing in the stock market**, and **doing chores**

* Although it is just a small family business, he is able to **balance personal life and work**. I think that's why he truly enjoys it
