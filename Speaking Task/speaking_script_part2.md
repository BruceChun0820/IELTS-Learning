# Wild animal

*Describe a wild animal that you want to know more about*

## full script

> If I could learn about any animal, I would go with dolphins. 
>
> Because they are fascinating and mysterious.
>
> I would be thrilled to learn more about dolphins in the future and I have been familiar with dolphins ==for ages growing up==. 
>
>  Since they were featured in so many cartoons and movies for kids because they are adorable. 
>
> I vividly remember the first time I saw dolphins. It was during a visit to an aquarium, where I watched them swimming ==gracefully== in the huge tank.
>
> Suddenly, they started jumping and playing together, almost like they were performing a show.
>
> It was one of the most ==spectacular== and fascinating experiences I’ve ever had, especially because I had never seen dolphins in real life before.
>
> There are two main reasons I would ==be stoked to== learn more about them.
>
> Firstly, they are well-known to be incredibly intelligent creatures.
>
> There are ==tales== of dolphins saving people from shark attacks and even saving people from ==drowning==.
>
> This is ==compelling stuff==.
>
> Some biologists even ==hypothesize== that they're capable of feeling ==compassion== and ==empathy==.
>
> So they are really unlike any other animal. 
>
> It would be really cool to learn more about their behaviour and the way their brains work.
>
> Secondly, I consider myself an ==environmentally-conscious== person and I am passionate about protecting animals.
>
> Some dolphin species are endangered. It would be ==devastating== if dolphins were lost to history because of their habitats being destroyed by humans.
>
> So if I could learn more about these majestic creatures I would definitely try to ==raise awareness about== protecting them allowing them to thrive in their natural environments.
