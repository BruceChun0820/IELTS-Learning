## Part3

*Do you think it’s important to develop children’s talents?*

* Yes, I think it's very important to develop children's talents
* **identifying and nurturing** their **natural abilities** at an early age can help them build confidence and motivation
* For example, if a child shows a gift for music, giving them the right training can make them more **engaged and enthusiastic**
* Besides, developing talents also benefits their future. Some children may even **turn their skills into career**, or at least enjoy them as **lifelong hobbies**

*Why do some people like to show their talents online?*

* I think there are two reasons
* first, social media gives them **a platform to express themselves** and share what they are good at with **wider audience**
* secondly, some people may even regard it as an opportunity to **develop a career**.
* For instance, talented gamers and singers can **attract sponsors** and turn **their hobbies into professional work** 

*Do you think artists with talents should focus on their talents?*

* Yes, I do
* talent is a rare gift, and if they **develop it further**, they can **create more outstanding works of art**
* So focusing on their talents allows them to **reach their full potential** and **inspire others**
* Of course, it is also important for artists to **balance their passion with practical needs**, like making a living
* But overall, focusing on their talents is the best effective way to **make the most of what they have**

*Is it possible for us to know whether children who are 3 or 4 years old will become musicians and painters when they grow up?*

* I would say it is definitely **impossible to predict** for sure
* At the age of three or four, children **are just starting to explore** the world, and their interests can change quickly
* Just because a child likes drawing or singing at that age **dose not mean they will necessarily become a painter or musician** in the future
* But **early exposure** can be a sign. If children **show strong interest and talent** **very early on**, and their parents give them the right training and encouragement
* then it is more likely that they **will continue down that path**

*Why do people like to watch talent shows?*

* I think there are two reasons
* First, they are **entertaining**
* Audiences can **watch different kinds of performances**, such as singing, dancing or even comedy, **all in one program**, which is both exciting and relaxing
* Secondly, these shows allow viewers to **relate to ordinary people**. Because many **participants** are amateurs rather than professional artists
* So their stories are quite inspiring and show that anyone can achieve something if they are somewhat talented and work hard
