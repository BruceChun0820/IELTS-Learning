## Part3

*Why should we protect wild animals?*

* I believe protecting wild animals is crucial for two reasons
* first of all, they play an important role in **maintaining the balance of ecosystems**
* if one species goes **extinct**, it could **disrupt** the food chain and lead to unexpected consequences in the natural world
* Secondly, wild animals contribute to scentific research and discoveries.
* For instance, studying certain species has helped scientists **develop new medicines** or better understand human biology

*Why are some people more willing to protect wild animals than others?*

* I think it mainly **depends on people's awareness**
* some individuals are **more conscious of** environmental issues and understand the importance of wildlife associated with our planet
* On the other hand, some people are less interested because they **don't feel directly affected**
* They may see wildlife protection as something **distant or unrelated** to their daily lives

*Do you think it’s important to take children to the zoo to see animals?*

* Yes, I believe it is quite important
* zoos give children a rare opportunity to **observe wild animals up close**
* which can **spark their curiosity** about nature and encourage them to learn more about **animals and environment protection**
* However, in **low-quality zoos**, animals are kept in poor conditions, it might **send the wrong message**
* But overall, a **well-managed zoo** can play an **educational and inspirational** role in a child's develoment.

*Why do some people attach more importance to protecting rare animals?*

* I think it is because rare animals are more **vulnerable and more likely to become extinct** without proper conservation
* Some people feel a strong sense of responsibility to preserve biodiversity
* especially when they realize that human activities like **pollution and deforestation** have caused the decline in some species 

*Should people educate children to protect wild animals?*

* Yes, I believe it is necessary to teach children about the importance of wild animals
* early education can raise their awareness about environmental issues and help understand the role ecosystem plays in our planet
* If they **grow up with a strong environmental mindset**
* they will be more willing to take action and make more sustainable choices to **preserve endangered species** and **avoid damaging natural environments**

*Is it more important to protect wild animals or the environment?*

* I think **both are equally important**, as wildlife and the environment are **closely interconnected**
* If we only protect animals but destroy their habitats, **they won't survive**
* On the other hand, if we protect the environment but ignore the species living in it, the ecosystem will still be **unbalanced**
* So in my opinion, both are equally important and **should be protected together**
