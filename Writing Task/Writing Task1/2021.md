## Sample

The two maps compare Beechwood Farm in 1950 and its current layout.

Overall, the farm has **shifted its primary function from agricultural production to recreation and tourism**. More facilities have been introduced for visitors, and the original tracks have been upgraded into proper roads.

The **sheep grazing area** in the top-right corner of the 1950 map has been replaced by a camping field in the center, solar panels in the top-right, and two parking lots -- **one on the right-hand side** and **another beside the main road on the left**. In addition, a farm shop has been added  in fruit tree area, opposite the left parking lot, while the fruit trees, soft fruit fields, and vegetable **plot** have basically **remained unchanged**.

The farmhouse and the chicken area beside the river have not changed. By contrast, the barn has **been relocated to** the southern end of **trianglar road junction**, between the vegetable field and the chicken area, while its original site has been **converted into** holiday cottages providing accommodation for guests. Moreover, the highway located in top-left corner and the river **surrounding** the farm have also **remained the same as 1950**.
