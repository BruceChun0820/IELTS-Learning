## Sample

> The bar chart compares the proportions of households living in owned and rented accommodation in England and Wales between 1918 and 2011.

> **Overall**, while renting was the **dominant form of housing** in the early part of the period, **home ownership** gradually increased and overtook renting from 1971 onwards.

> In 1918, around 77% of households lived in rented accommodation, whereas only about 23% owned their homes. Similar proportions were **recorded** in 1939 and 1953, with roughly **one-third of homes** being owned and **two-thirds rented**. By 1961, ownership had risen to just over 40%, while the share of rented accommodation fell to around 60%.

> **A significant change occurred** in 1971, when the figures for **both categories** became equal at 50%. After that, the proportion of households owning their homes rose sharply to 60% in 1981 and reached nearly 70% by 1991 and 2001. However, in 2011, the percentage of owned accommodation experienced a slight decline to around 64%, whereas renting increased **modestly** to about 36%.
