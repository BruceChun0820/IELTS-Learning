## Essay Sample

The bar chart shows the proportions of residents in five Australian cities who , during the previous four weeks, visited a cafe for coffee or tea, bought instant coffee, or purchased fresh coffee. 

Overall, visiting cafe was the most common choice in all five cities, while instant coffee was consistently more popular than fresh coffee, which was generally the least common option.

In Sydney, about 61% of  residents went to a cafe, compared with roughly 46% who bought instant coffee and around 43% who purchased fresh coffee. Melbourne ==followed a similar pattern== but with ==marginally== higher cafe use: just over 60% visited cafe, while approximately 48% bought instant coffee and a little above 40% bought fresh coffee.

Brisbane also favored cafe at about 55%, ==narrowly== ahead of instant coffee at around 53%, whereas fresh coffee was much less popular at roughly 34%. Adelaide was distinctive in that cafe visits and instant coffee purchases were both close to 50%, with fresh coffee at about 35%. In Hobart, more than 60% of residents went to cafe, around 54% bought instant coffee and about 38% bought fresh coffee.
