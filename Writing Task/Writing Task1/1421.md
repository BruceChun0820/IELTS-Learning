## Sample

The bar chart compares a country's export earnings in five product categories between 2015 and 2016, while the table **indicates** the percentage change in export value for each category during the same period.

Overall, petroleum products and engineered goods **generated** the highest export earnings in both years, whereas textiles experienced the most significant growth **in value**.

In detail, the export value of petroleum products rose slightly from around $60 billion in 2015 to approximately \$62 billion in 2016, **representing a 3% increase**. A similar pattern was seen in engineered goods, whose earnings **climbed** about \$57 billion in 2015 **to** just over \$60 billion, **an 8.5% growth**.

By contrast, gems and jewellery were **the only category to experience a decline**, dropping from roughly \$42 billion in 2015 to about \$40 billion in 2016, a 5.18% decrease. Agricultural products showed little change, increasing **marginally** by 0.81%, with values remaining just about \$30 billion in both years. Finally, textiles **recorded** the most **substantial** rise, surging from around \$25 billion to over \$30 billion, which **corresponds** to a 15.24% growth.
