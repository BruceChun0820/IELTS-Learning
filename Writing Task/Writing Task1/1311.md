## Sample

> The two maps illustrate changes made to the road access around a city hospital between 2007 and 2010.

> Overall, by 2010, significant improvements had been made to the hospital’s transport infrastructure, including the addition of a new bus station and a separate public car park, while the main roads and hospital building remained basically unchanged.

> On the western side, **two roundabouts were added at the junctions of Hospital Road with the Ring Road and City Road respectively**, helping to ease traffic congestion around the hospital. The previous bus stops along Hospital Road were removed and replaced by a large bus station, which **could be accessed via** these roundabouts.

> On the eastern side, a new public car park was built to the east of the hospital, along the ring road. Consequently, **the former combined car park** was converted into a staff-only parking area, separating the two groups and likely mitigating congestion around the hospital area.
