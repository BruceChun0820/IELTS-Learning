Overall, the procedure consists of nine steps, which can be divided into three ==broad phases==: the growth and harvesting of bamboo, the processing of raw baboo into fibres, and finally the production of fabric and finished garments.

In the ==initial stage==, bamboo plants are cultivated in spring and harvested in the autumn. Once collected, the ==bamboo stems== are cut into strips using saws. These strips are ==subsequently== crushed to form a liquid pulp. The pulp then ==undergoes a filtering process== in which long fibres are separated from the liquid. 

Afterwards, water and amine oxide are added in order to soften the fibres. ==Following this==, the softened fibres are spun to produce yarn. The yarn is then woven into fabric, which ==constitutes== the essential raw material for ==textile manufacturing==. In the final stage, the fabric is used to ==create== ==a range of== clothing products such as socks, shirts and other garments for the consumer market.
