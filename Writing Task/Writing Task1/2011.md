## Essay sample

The tables illutrate changes in the population of New York City and its five districts, including Manhattan, Brooklyn, Bronx, Queens, and Staten Island, over a two-hundred-year period.

Overall, the proportion of Manhattan's population declined dramatically, while the population of the other four districts increased significantly. Meanwhile, the total population of the city rose remarkably over the period.

The total population of New York City increased significantly from 79,216 in 1800 to 3,437,202 in 1900, and then exceeded eight million by 2000. The proportion of Manhattan's population showed a consistent downward trend, falling from 76% in 1800 to 54% in 1900 amd further to 19 % in 2000. ==In termas of absolute numbers==, Manhattan's population rose from 60,515 in 1800 to 1,850,093 in 1900, before dropping to 1,538,096 in 2000.

As the proportion of Manhattan declined, the share of the other four districts rose substantially, from 24% in 1800 to 46% in 1900, and then to 81% in 2000. ==In absolute terms==, their combined population grew remarkably from merely 18,701 in 1800 to 1,587,109 in 1900, before reaching 6,471,089 in 2000.
