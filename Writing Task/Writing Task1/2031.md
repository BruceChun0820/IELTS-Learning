Overall, adults **aged 18-64** made up the majority of library members, while both children's and adult fiction dominated the loan categories. In addition, the total number of loans experienced a steady upward trend over the given period.

In the table, **both** children's fiction and adult fiction accounted for 38% **each**, whereas children's non-fiction made up merely 6% and adult non-fiction **stood at** 13%.
