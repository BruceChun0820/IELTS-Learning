## Essay Question

*Some people think that competition at work, at school and in daily life is a good thing. Others believe that we should try to cooperate more, rather than competing against each other.*

**Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.*

## Essay Sample

In modern society, competition and cooperation are two key forces that shape people's behaviour at school, at work and in daily life. Some argue that competition motivates individuals work harder and achieve greater results, while I believe that cooperation is more important for long-term development.

First of all, cooperation plays a critical role in tackling complex challenges. ==Grand undertakings== cannot be accomplished by individuals alone but require teamwork and shared responsibility. For example, in the field of scientific research, collaboration among experts from various ==disciplines== often leads to breakthroughs, as they can share knowledge and resources. 

What's more, cooperation fosters a supportive environment that encourages innovation and emotional well-being. For instance, in a large company, different departments need to collaborate to ensure smooth operations, and in sports, teammates must coordinate with each other to succeed. In daily life, cooperation also strengthens friendships and unnecessary conflicts, which ==benefits both individuals and society as a whole.==

Admittedly, competition can sometimes stimulate progress. Students who compete with their peers may study harder, and companies in a competitive market may innovate faster. However, excessive ==rivalry== can also generate stress and ==hostility==, leaving individuals isolated and even harming relationships. By contrast, cooperation creates harmony and lays a stronger foundation for sustainable success.

In conclusion, although competition has certain advantages, cooperation contributes more to both personal and social development. Therefore, I believe that cooperation out weighs competition in most ==circumstances==.
