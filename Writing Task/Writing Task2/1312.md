# Essay Question

*Living in a country where you have to speak a foreign language can cause serious social problems, as well as practical problems.*

*To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?*

*Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.*

*Write at least 250 words.*

## Sample

> Living in a country where one has to speak a foreign language can undoubtedly bring both social and practical challenges. While learning the local language helps **integration** to some extent, I strongly agree that the **inability** to communicate fluently often leads to a range of difficulties in daily life and social interaction.

> From a practical perspective, language **barriers** can make even simple tasks **frustrating** and **time-consuming**. People who cannot speak the **host country**’s language may struggle with essential activities such as shopping, using public transport, or **seeking medical care**. For example, new immigrants in France often find it hard to access healthcare services because medical professionals and documents are primarily in French. Such communication gaps can lead to misunderstandings or even dangerous mistakes in treatment. In addition, limited language skills may **restrict employment opportunities**, preventing individuals from **obtaining stable income** or developing their careers.

> On a social level, the inability to express oneself fluently can easily result in **isolation** and cultural misunderstanding. When people cannot communicate naturally with locals, they may find it difficult to make friends, participate in community events, or understand social **norms**. This often causes feelings of loneliness and **exclusion**. For instance, international students in Japan frequently report experiencing **social anxiety** and **homesickness** because they cannot fully integrate into **Japanese-speaking groups**. **Over time**, this lack of social connection may **negatively affect** their mental well-being.

> Admittedly, modern technology and **bilingual** services have made communication abroad easier than before. Translation apps, for instance, can help with basic daily tasks. However, these tools cannot replace **genuine** human interaction or cultural understanding, both of which require language **proficiency**.

> In conclusion, living in a foreign-language environment can create serious social and practical problems, **ranging from** daily **inconveniences** to emotional and cultural isolation. Therefore, acquiring at least basic proficiency in the local language is essential for anyone hoping to live abroad successfully.
