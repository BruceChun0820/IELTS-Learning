## Question

*In some cultures, children are often told that they can achieve anything if they try hard enough.*

## Sample

**Introduction**

In some societies, **hard work is considered** a primary factor in children’s education, and they are often told that if they make enough effort, they can achieve any goal. Although such encouragement may motivate young students to devote themselves to academic tasks and aspire to higher achievement, I believe that **overemphasizing this notion may damage children’s confidence** when they fail despite their best efforts.

**Body 1 – Advantages**

To begin with, the message that effort leads to success is widely used in education systems around the world. Parents and teachers often set goals for children and consistently encourage them to **strive** for higher levels of achievement. This belief can significantly **motivate students to concentrate on their studies** and become more resilient when facing challenges. Moreover, it can **foster a strong work ethic**, which is valuable not only in education but also in professional life.

**Body 2 – Disadvantages**

However, success does not depend on individual effort alone. Other factors such as supportive environments, collaboration, external recognition, and sometimes even good fortune also play indispensable roles. If children are guided by the misleading idea that **hard work always guarantees success**, they may develop **unrealistic expectations**. Consequently, when they fail to reach overly ambitious goals, their confidence may **collapse**, leading to stress and even mental health issues. For example, psychologists have pointed out that a significant proportion of teenage anxiety stems from **parental pressure and unattainable goals**.

**Body 3 – Balanced approach**

Therefore, while hard work should certainly be valued, it should not **be portrayed as** the **sole** **determinant** of achievement. Instead, children should also be taught problem-solving skills, interpersonal abilities, and the importance of cooperation and **seizing opportunities**. By **integrating multiple factors into education**, schools and parents can **cultivate** well-rounded and resilient young people who are better prepared for the uncertainties of real life.

**Conclusion**

In conclusion, hard work undoubtedly plays a crucial role in helping individuals pursue their goals, but success also requires **a combination of other supports**. Parents and teachers who encourage children to work hard while adopting a **balanced and realistic perspective** can foster healthier attitudes and greater long-term achievements.
