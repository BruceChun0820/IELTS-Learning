## Question

*In many countries, primary and secondary schools close for two months or more in the summer holidays.*

*What is the value of long school holidays?*

*What are the arguments in favour of shorter school holidays?*

## Sample

In many societies, elementary and middle school students are allowed to have a long holiday lasting two months or more. However, some argue that students should not have so much free time, as it may not benefit their academic careers. I strongly disagree with such a view and believe there are many reasons why holiday is both meaningful and positive for children.

First of all, long school holidays provide valuable opportunities for students to engage in ==non-academic learning== and practical experience. Instead of ==being confined to textbooks and examinations==, children can join summer camps, take part in volunteer work, or learn new skills such as music, art, or sports. These experience not only ==broaden their horizons== but also ==cultivate qualities== such as teamwork, independence, and problem-solving, which are difficult to develop within a traditional classroom setting.

Moreover, ==under today's fast-paced study schedules and lifestyle==, summer holidays offer invaluable time for children to enjoy leisure with their family and friends. In this sense, they can share their concerns and challenges encountered ==in the process of growing up==, and reinforce bonds with their parents, who are often buried in work. For instance, psychologists point out that the mental well-being of children and teenagers relies heavily on communication and understanding within the family, particularly with parents.

Admittedly, academic performance plays a critical role in students' ==future prospects==. However, ==unceasing immersion in== knowledge and examination would be a poor way to shape their personality and could even ==stifle== imagination and creativity. A proper summer holiday provides an opportunity for children  to take a break, explore the world beyond school, and discover the kind of life they ==aspire to==.

In conclusion, I do not believe that current summer holidays are too long for students. On the contrary, I firmly hold that adequate leisure time not only foster students' creativity and innovation but also strengthens their family relationships, which are both essential for shaping a ==well-rounded== and balanced personality.
