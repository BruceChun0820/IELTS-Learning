## Question

*Some people believe that it is best to accept a bad situation, such as an unsatisfactory job or shortage of money. Others argue that it is better to try and improve such situations.* 

*Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.*

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

## Sample

**Introduction**

In recent years, there has been wide debate over how people should respond to adversity. Some believe that it is best to accept difficult circumstances such as an unsatisfactory job or financial hardship, while others argue that individuals should make efforts to change and improve them. I firmly agree with the latter view, as persistence in overcoming difficulties has always been the driving force behind human progress and innovation.

**Body Paragraph 1 – Against passive acceptance**

If people simply accept adversity, disappointment and frustration may gradually destroy their confidence and courage, which could harm their mental well-being or even lead to **psychological disorders**. However, most difficulties are not irreversible. For instance, during World War II, the United Kingdom suffered heavy attacks from Germany, creating fear and hopelessness. Nevertheless, the government and citizens stood together and resisted with perseverance and determination, which ultimately contributed to victory. In this case, resilience and effort can **turn even the worst situations around**.

**Body Paragraph 2 – Support for improvement and effort**

It is often said that *what doesn’t kill you makes you stronger*. Almost every successful individual has experienced **setbacks** before **achieving great accomplishments**. For example, many scientists went through countless failed experiments before producing **groundbreaking** inventions that improved human welfare. Similarly, **in everyday life**, people can learn and grow from challenges rather than succumbing to them. Taking action to solve problems not only **strengthens one’s ability** but also **cultivates** **optimism** and **perseverance**.

**Body Paragraph 3 – Acknowledging limitations but emphasizing action**

Admittedly, there are cases where people lack the capacity to change **large-scale** social issues or personal **misfortunes** immediately. However, this does not mean they should simply give up. Society should **encourage cooperation and mutual support**, where those with greater resources or power assist others in overcoming **obstacles**. Such **collective efforts** can gradually create a more supportive and equitable environment.

**Conclusion**

In conclusion, **adversity and hardship** are rarely **insurmountable**. People should remain calm, confident, and **proactive** when facing difficulties, while society should foster a culture that values resilience and **mutual assistance**. Only through such attitudes can individuals and communities achieve lasting progress and strength.
