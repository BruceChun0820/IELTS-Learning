## Question

*Some people say that the main environmental problem of our time is the loss of particular species of plants and animals. Others say that there are more important environmental problems.*

## Sample

Nowadays, environmental issues have become one of the most immediate global **concerns**. Some argue that the **extinction** of certain animals and plants is the most serious environmental problem, while others believe that there are more urgent challenges to address. I personally agree that the loss of species is the most critical issue, as it directly **disrupts** ecosystem and **threathens** human survival in the long term.

To begin with, the disappearance of specific species can cause irreversible damage to the balance of ecosyctems. Every **origanism** plays a unique role in **maintaining the stability** of the natural environment. For instance, the decline of certain bird species has been linked to increased pest populations, which in turn affects agriculture and food security. Therefore, the decrease or even extiction of plants and annimals can create **a chain reaction** that eventually harms people's livelihoods.

Furthermore, the loss of biodiversity can weaken the plant's ability to recover from environmental changes. Forests, coral reefs, and wetlands -- all of which depend on diverse species -- act as natural protectors against floods, climat change, and soil erosion. Once species within these systems disappear, their ability to absorb carbon dioxide and purify air and water is reduced. In other words, preventing species loss is effective way to combat global warming and protect natural resources.

Admittedly, other problems such as plasitc pollution and excessive carbon emissions are also serious. However, many of these issues are interconnected with biodiversity loss. For example, marine pollution leads to the death of countless fish species, which again highlight that species extinction is at the heart of most environmental crises.

In conclusion, while there are several environmental problems facing the world today, the loss of plants and animals is the most fundamental one. Protecting biodiversity is not only about saving wildlife, but also about ensuring the survival of human life on Earth.
