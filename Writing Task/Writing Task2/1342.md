## Question

*In spite of the advances made in agriculture, many people around the world still go hungry.*

*Why is this the case?*

*What can be done about this problem?*

## Sample

In recent decades, technological advancement and automation have resolved many problems in various industries. However, despite significant improvements in agriculture, **a large number of** people around the world still **suffer from** hunger. In my opinion, this phenomenon **stems from** a range of complex factors, and it requires not only **effective governmental intervention** but also **greater public awareness and participation**.

One of the main reasons why hunger **persists** is the unequal distribution of resources. Although modern farming methods have greatly increased **crop yields**, the benefits are not **evenly** shared among nations. In many developing countries, poor infrastructure and inadequate storage facilities lead to massive food waste. Meanwhile, **political instability** and **corruption** can prevent **food aid** from reaching **those most in need**.  For example, in some parts of Africa, **donated grains** often fail to reach local communities due to transportation difficulties or **mismanagement** by local authorities.

Another **contributing factor** is **economic disparity**. Many low-income families simply cannot afford sufficient food even when supplies are available. Urbanization and industrialization have also reduced **arable land**, forcing many rural residents to move to cities and abandon traditional farming. As a result, local production declines while dependence on imported food increases, which makes people more **vulnerable** to **global market fluctuations**.

To tackle this issue, government should prioritize improving infrastructure and **governance** in the agricultural sector. Investment in storage facilities, transportation, and **fair trade systems** can help ensure that food is distributed more efficiently. International organizations can also collaborate to provide financial and technical support to coutries that struggle with **food shortages and insecurity**. On an individual level, people can contribute by reducing food waste and supporting sustainable farming practices through **conscious consumption**.

In conclusion, the **persistence** of hunger despite advances in agriculture can be attributed to **uneven** distribution, poverty, and poor management. **By combining** governmental efforts with public participation, this global challenge can be gradually mitigated.
